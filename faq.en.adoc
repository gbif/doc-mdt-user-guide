[[faq]]
== FAQ

For questions about preparing your data, such as removing controls or rarefying, see <<before_you_start>>.

[discrete]
[[faq_what]]
=== What does the MDT do?

It converts a DNA metabarcoding dataset – an OTU table with sample and taxonomy information – into a <<dwc-a,Darwin Core Archive>> and publishes it on GBIF.org. You could do this by hand following the guide https://doi.org/10.35035/doc-vf1a-nr22[Publishing DNA-derived data through biodiversity data platforms]; the MDT automates it.

[discrete]
[[faq_templates]]
=== Are there data templates?

Yes, see <<templates>>.

[discrete]
[[faq_metagenomics]]
=== Can I use the MDT for metagenomic data?

No. The MDT is for metabarcoding data, where specific DNA regions were amplified by PCR (for example COI, ITS, 12S or 18S). Metagenomic (shotgun) data without such a PCR step cannot be processed. "16S amplicon" data is metabarcoding data, even though it is often called metagenomics.

[discrete]
[[faq_markers]]
=== Which markers can I publish?

Any marker (barcode region). Publish one dataset per marker.

[discrete]
[[faq_convert_only]]
=== Can I use the MDT only to convert my data?

Yes. You can download the Darwin Core Archive in step 6 and publish it elsewhere, or download the BIOM files that the MDT creates in steps 3 and 6.

[discrete]
[[faq_doi]]
=== Does my dataset get a DOI?

Yes. GBIF gives your dataset a DOI when it is published. You do not need to request one.

[discrete]
[[faq_update]]
=== Can I update my dataset after publishing?

Yes. Edit it in the MDT and publish it again. It keeps its page and DOI on GBIF.org. See <<update>>.

[discrete]
[[faq_taxonomy_update]]
=== Will GBIF update the taxonomy of my sequences later?

Not at present. Reference databases improve over time, so sequences that cannot be named today may be identifiable later. That is why all sequences should be shared, including unidentified ones.

GBIF is working on this: its SPLICE project, which aims to fully integrate environmental DNA (eDNA) data into GBIF's infrastructure, includes continuous taxonomic reinterpretation of shared sequences against updated reference libraries.

[discrete]
[[faq_bugs]]
=== Where can I report a bug or request a feature?

Click *Report a bug* or *Request a feature* in the MDT, or create an issue on https://github.com/gbif/metabarcoding-data-toolkit-ui/issues[GitHub].
