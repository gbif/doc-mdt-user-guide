=== Abstract

The Metabarcoding Data Toolkit (MDT) is a web tool that converts DNA metabarcoding data (OTU tables with sample and taxonomy information) into Darwin Core Archives and publishes them through GBIF and OBIS. This guide explains, step by step, how to prepare your data, process it in the MDT and publish it.
