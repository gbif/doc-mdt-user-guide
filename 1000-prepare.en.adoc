[[preparation_structure]]
== Prepare your data

Prepare your data and metadata before you open the MDT. Most problems in the MDT start with how the files were prepared.

. Make the decisions in <<before_you_start>>.
. Put your data into the tables described in <<file_rules>>, using the fields in <<req_recom>>.
. Prepare and write your dataset description, etc. as described in <<prepare_metadata>>.
